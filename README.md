# Acoustics Resources

This Repository contains resources or links to resources loosely in the field of bioacoustics, 
insect bioacoustics, computational bioacoustics, and ecoacoustics for reference. Feel free to 
add more resources and send in a pull request, or contact me at arpito[at]iisc.ac.in to talk 
more about these.

## List of Interesting Publications

Here are a few publications that I found interesting during the course of my PhD and hope that others will 
also appreciate them:
- [Acoustic Communication in Insects and Anurans](https://books.google.co.in/books?hl=en&lr=&id=0ySUuQTNMpkC&oi=fnd&pg=PR7&dq=gerhardt+and+huber+2002&ots=uYxXu1hwlm&sig=mot2J143QaSREeyfbtMHj7bduEQ&redir_esc=y#v=onepage&q&f=false) - Probably the best/widely known resource/book on animal communication in Insects.
- [Resonant song recognition and evolution of acoustic communication in crickets](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2589004224029225) - Talks about a peculiar cricket *Anurogryllus muticus* and insights it provides into the neural evolution of acoustic communication.
- [Aggresiveness, Territoriality and Sexual Behaviour in Field Crickets](https://brill.com/view/journals/beh/17/2-3/article-p130_4.xml?ebody=Abstract%2FExcerpt) - Nice description of aggressive behaviour in crickets.
- [Mating behaviour and mating songs of sandy beach-dwelling ground crickets](https://jor.pensoft.net/article/108536/) - Study on mating behaviours and calls of crickets, nice description of behaviour and contains various song parameters in cricket calls typically used to describe species/call types.
- [Amazonian soundscapes: unravelling the secrets of insect acoustic niches in diverse habitats](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/view/6292/) - Uses ecoacoustic indices to study insect calling across different habitats.
- [Acoustic monitoring of singing insects](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/4218202) - Early attempts at building insect classifiers.
- [Effect of temperature, humidity, and age on stridulation](https://academic.oup.com/aesa/article-abstract/68/3/607/80838) - Tests the stridulation rates in crickets in lab conditions against abiotic and biotic variables.
- [Temperature dependent stridulation in four-eyed bark beetle](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1226861519304194) - Another interesting paper showing stridulation is affected by temperature in other species of insects as well.
- [Tree crickets optimize the acoustics of baffles to exaggerate mate attraction signals](https://elifesciences.org/articles/32763) - Cool paper on tool use by crickets for sound amplification.
- [Extensive data engineering to the rescue: building a multi-species katydid detector](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11070257/#s6) - Building neural network based species classifiers for insects using limited data.
- [Effects of moonlight on calling activity in neotropical insects](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11070251/) - Another paper from the same group above, using the classifier to study insect calling behaviour w.r.t. moonlight.
- [Biologically meaningful moonlight measures and their application in ecological research](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00265-022-03287-2) - Talks about moonlit package in R that can be used to estimate moonlight and moonphase at different locations and elevations.
- [Survival Sounds in Insects](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/ecology-and-evolution/articles/10.3389/fevo.2021.641740/full) - Cool paper that talks about different kinds of sounds produced by insects across major orders with reference to predator defense, e.g., aposematic sounds, jamming signals/interference, startling sounds, etc.
- [Anti bat ultrasound production in moths](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2117485119) - Cool paper showing defensive sounds by moths to avoid being predated by bats.
- [Acoustically eavesdropping bat predators take longer to capture katydid prey signalling in aggregation](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article-pdf/224/10/jeb233262/2072076/jeb233262.pdf) - Another interesting predator avoidance strategy paper, where they show chorusing might be beneficial to avoid predators.
- [Acoustic monitoring for tropical insect conservation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12180399/) - lays down a roadmap for insect conservation using acoustic monitoring.
- [Data driven sampling strategies for fine-tuning bird detection models](https://pubs.aip.org/asa/jasa/article/159/6/4891/3393438) - Interesting paper on strategies for efficient annotations of audio files to build classifiers.

## Other Resources

Here are some particularly interesting videos/resources I have found useful across the years on various topics that 
might be of interest to budding bioacousticians and/or computational ecologists:
- [Sci-kit Learn User Guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/user_guide.html) - Probably the best written and easy to understand guide on various algorithms typically used in machine learning and recommendations/best practices for their usage.
- [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions) - Great website to get started with hands on machine learning projects and competitions that you can participate in. Remember to take it slow and have fun!
- [3Blue1Brown Series on Neural Networks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aircAruvnKk&list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi) - Very interesting video series to delve deeper into how modern machine learning algorithms work.
- [3Blue1Brown Fourier Series Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r6sGWTCMz2k) - Cool video on Fourier Series - very interesting if you're into signal processing.

## Public Datasets

Here are a few public datasets that I have found over the years and anyone can download and try to practice 
or hone their skills on:
- [InsectSet459](https://zenodo.org/records/18554693) - A large dataset for automatic acoustic identification of insects (Orthoptera and Cicadidae). There's an associated publication [here](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-07123-4#Sec6) describing the dataset.
- [Sounds of Neotropical Katydids from Barro Colorado Island, Panama](https://zenodo.org/records/10594016) - Collection of annotated audio files for neotropical katydids. Dataset used in the previously quoted paper (extensive data engineering to the rescue).
- [Neotropical Forest Soundscapes with Katydid call ID](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.zw3r2288b) - Another dataset from Barro Colorado Island, Panama, also from the same paper as above.
